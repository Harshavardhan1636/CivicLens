About CivicLens

DevOps Lab — Full CI/CD Project

Problem: Potholes, overflowing bins and broken streetlights go unreported because there is no simple local channel. Helplines are slow and give no tracking.

Solution: CivicLens lets residents report an issue with a photo and location, see every report on a live map, upvote the urgent ones, and follow each case from Open to Resolved.

How it is built: Python Flask and SQLite, Leaflet maps, Docker and docker-compose, and a Jenkins pipeline (checkout, test, build, deploy) with pytest as the quality gate.
